What this is

About Burnout Pulse

A small tool. Built with one job in mind: help you notice how you're doing before the week tells you the hard way.

What it does

Once a week, you answer six short questions about how the week treated you. The app turns those into a single score and tracks the trend over time. If a score is heavy, it gives you specific guidance for the dimensions that are most strained. The coach is a space to think out loud, with the safety net of crisis resources if a conversation needs them.

It takes about two minutes. It is deliberately small.

The six dimensions

The score is built from six axes drawn from the burnout research literature, in particular the work of Christina Maslach. The wording is adapted to specialty, but the underlying axes stay the same so your trend remains comparable.

Your privacy

Everything in Burnout Pulse stays in this browser. Your check-ins, your coach conversations, your journal, your friends list. None of it is sent to a server in this build.

That means it's private from anyone except whoever has access to this device. It also means clearing your browser data erases your history, and using a different browser or device starts you fresh. It is not a clinical record system, and it shouldn't be treated as one.

What it isn't

Not a diagnostic tool. Not a substitute for talking to a clinician, a counsellor, or a trusted colleague. The coach is supportive, but it's not a therapist, and when it senses you're in crisis it will say so and point you to people who are.
